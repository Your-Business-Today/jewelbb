**Who to contact.** Jeremy Ferendinos, Director, handles all data protection matters. Email info@jewelbb.co.uk, phone 0208 109 1015, or write to the address above.

This notice covers our website, enquiries and quotes, our building projects, phone calls, neighbours and visitors, and business contacts, including subcontractors. If you work for us or apply to, a separate staff privacy notice applies.

## What we collect

- **Enquiries and quotes:** your name, email, phone number and what you tell us about your project. Your property address, drawings and budget when you give them to us.
- **Projects:** contracts, drawings, specifications, site photographs and videos, progress notes, variations, valuations and payment records. Site photos are of the works and may show parts of your home. We try not to capture people or personal items.
- **Phone calls:** calls to our main number may be answered by an automated assistant. Calls are recorded and transcribed, and a written summary is sent to our team so the right person can call you back.
- **Neighbours and visitors:** contact details where needed for party wall matters, access or site safety.
- **Business contacts:** names, job titles and contact details of architects, engineers, surveyors, insurers, suppliers and subcontractors.
- **Subcontractors and site operatives:** names, contact and company details, tax details for the Construction Industry Scheme (CIS), right-to-work checks, training and qualification cards, emergency contacts and payment details.
- **Website:** basic technical data such as your IP address, needed to run the site securely.

**Health and accessibility needs.** If we are designing or adapting your home around a disability or health condition, such as a lift, level access or an adapted bathroom, we hold the details you give us about those needs. We use them only to design and build the work, and keep them with the project record. Health details are optional: tell us only what we need to design the work. We ask for your explicit consent before we record them.

**Where else we get information.** Some information comes from other people: architects and designers who introduce or tender projects, insurers and loss adjusters who instruct work, party wall surveyors, building control and warranty providers, and family members or representatives who contact us for you. People who appear in site photos have not given their details to us directly; we keep such images to a minimum.

## Why we use it, and our lawful basis

- **To prepare quotes and carry out building work:** performing a contract with you, or taking steps you asked for before one. For people who did not engage us, such as neighbours, visitors and staff of other firms, our legitimate interest in delivering the work.
- **To keep project records, including photos:** our contract with you and our legitimate interest in evidencing our work, supporting building safety and responding to claims.
- **To meet construction, health and safety, Building Regulations, Building Safety Act, CIS and tax duties:** legal obligation.
- **To engage and pay subcontractors and run our sites safely:** performing our contract with them, legal obligation, and our legitimate interest in safe, properly managed sites.
- **To answer calls through our automated assistant:** our legitimate interest in answering every call and passing it to the right person.
- **To run and secure our business:** legitimate interests. This includes using AI tools, mainly Claude (made by Anthropic), to read, sort, summarise and draft from our emails and documents, which can include your details. A member of our team checks the output. We never use AI or any automated process to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.
- **Health and accessibility details:** your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To show completed projects, testimonials and reviews on our website or social media:** only with your written consent. We identify projects by town, never by your address, and use your name only if you agree.

**Marketing.** We sometimes send emails about our services. To people at businesses we work with, we rely on our legitimate interest. To individual clients, we email only about similar services, and you can opt out when you give us your details and in every email. Anyone else hears from us only with consent. You can opt out at any time by replying or emailing info@jewelbb.co.uk.

**Do you have to give us your details?** We need your contact details, the property address and access arrangements to quote for and carry out building work. Without them we cannot take the job on. Everything else is optional.
